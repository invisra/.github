# Security Policy

Invisra takes the security of our software and services seriously. This policy
applies to every repository in the [`invisra`](https://github.com/invisra)
organization that does not publish its own `SECURITY.md`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues,
pull requests, or discussions.**

Instead, report them by email to **[security@invisra.ai](mailto:security@invisra.ai)**.

For sensitive reports, encrypt your message with our OpenPGP key:

- **Key:** <https://assets.invisra.ai/keys/security-openpgp.asc>
- **Fingerprint:** `C472 E638 2DF7 FB4A E64F  FB96 0FCB 0778 B7CC A454`

Our canonical policy and key details are published at
<https://invisra.ai/security> and
<https://invisra.ai/.well-known/security.txt> (RFC 9116).

## What to include

To help us triage quickly, please include as much of the following as you can:

- The affected repository, service, URL, or component and the version or commit.
- A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
- Steps to reproduce, proof-of-concept code, or a working exploit.
- Any relevant logs, screenshots, or configuration details.

## What to expect

- We will acknowledge your report and let you know the next steps.
- We will keep you informed as we investigate and work on a fix.
- We will credit you for the discovery once the issue is resolved, unless you
  prefer to remain anonymous.

## Coordinated disclosure

Please give us a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate before any
public disclosure, and avoid accessing or modifying other users' data,
degrading our services, or violating privacy while researching. We appreciate
your good-faith effort to keep Invisra and its users safe.
